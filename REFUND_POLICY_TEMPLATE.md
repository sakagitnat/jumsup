# Jumsup Refund Policy Template

This is a product-policy template, not legal advice. Review it for your jurisdiction before launch.

Recommended operational policy:
- Customers submit refund requests from Profile → Payments & Refunds.
- Requests are reviewed by an admin; refunds are not automatic.
- Full and partial refunds are technically supported.
- Refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method.
- A full refund can cancel the associated subscription immediately if the customer selected that option.
- A partial refund does not automatically cancel the subscription.
- If the refunded payment was the first payment that qualified a referral, the referral Pro bonus is revoked.
- Refund/dispute actions are retained in the payment and audit logs.
- Stripe processing time and bank posting time are outside Jumsup's direct control.
- Publish clear eligibility and time limits before accepting live payments.
